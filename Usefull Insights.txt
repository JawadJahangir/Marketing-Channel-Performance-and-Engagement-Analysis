 BUSINESS INSIGHTS REPORT
 Executive Summary

The analysis reveals that while the business is generating substantial traffic, a significant portion of it lacks meaningful engagement. The core issue is not traffic generation but traffic quality and conversion efficiency.

 Key Findings
1 High Traffic ≠ High Engagement
Some channels generate high sessions but low engaged sessions
Indicates poor targeting or irrelevant audience

Insight:
Traffic volume alone is a misleading metric

2 Engagement Rate is the True KPI
Engagement rate varies significantly across channels
High engagement channels deliver better value

 Insight:
Focus should shift from sessions → engagement

3 Low-Quality Channels Waste Budget
Channels with low engagement (<30%)
High non-engaged sessions

 Insight:
Marketing spend is being wasted on ineffective channels

4⃣ Time-Based Behavior Matters
User activity varies by hour
Peak hours offer better engagement opportunities

 Insight:
Campaign timing can significantly impact performance

5⃣ Non-Engaged Sessions = Hidden Loss
Large gap between sessions and engaged sessions

 Insight:
Represents lost opportunities and inefficient marketing

 Strategic Recommendations
1. Focus on High Engagement Channels
Allocate budget based on engagement rate
2. Reduce Low-Performing Channels
Optimize or eliminate poor-quality traffic sources
3. Optimize Campaign Timing
Run campaigns during peak engagement hours
4. Improve Targeting Strategy
Refine audience segmentation
5. Track Meaningful KPIs
Engagement rate > sessions
Conversion-focused metrics
Business Impact
Increased marketing ROI
Reduced wasted budget
Improved campaign efficiency
Better customer targeting
Final Conclusion

The business must transition from a traffic-driven strategy to an engagement-driven strategy to achieve sustainable growth and profitability.
